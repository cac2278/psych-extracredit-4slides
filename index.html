Is it credible?

Four quick checks before you use a source.

01
Check the date

Is it current? Has newer research changed what we know?

02
Verify elsewhere

Do other trustworthy sources report similar findings?

03
Check the author

Does the writer have relevant psychology or science-reporting expertise?

04
Check the format

Is it a book, peer-reviewed journal article, or news article? Did you find it through the library or on the open web?

Is it a social media post!? Is it AI-generated!?
